# Homepage design plan

The reference audit pointed to one clear correction: discovery should support the sale rather than delay it. The new rhythm is **attention → shop → proof → place → taste → story**.

1. Keep the cart-led hero, tighten it for a phone screen, and make `Shop the angdi` the primary action.
2. Add a snap-scrolling quick shelf before any long-form story.
3. Compress authenticity into a paper note with five clear proof points.
4. Replace the linear region rail with a code-native Karnataka map. Pulsing map pins select a region; the map shifts and scales while a parchment panel reveals the region in the same frame.
5. Add taste tabs as the second selling moment for customers who do not know a regional name.
6. Retain the existing story cards and story overlay as the emotional close.

Pinterest and the supplied references are mood research only. Shipping imagery must be original, supplied by the owner, or clearly licensed; watermarked images will not be used.
